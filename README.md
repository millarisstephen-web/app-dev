#  Grave of the Fireflies (1988)

> *"Why do fireflies have to die so soon?"* — Setsuko

Directed by **Isao Takahata** and animated by **Studio Ghibli**, *Grave of the Fireflies* is a heartbreaking World War II drama about two orphaned siblings—**Seita** and his little sister **Setsuko**—fighting to survive in war-torn Japan.

---

### Quick Info
* **Year:** 1988 | **Runtime:** 89 mins | **Studio:** Studio Ghibli

### Main Characters
| Character | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Seita** | Protective older brother trying to keep his sister safe |
| **Setsuko** | His innocent 4-year-old sister |

### Why It's Unforgettable
1.  Focuses on the real human cost of war rather than battles.
2.  A masterpiece you'll remember forever—just keep tissues nearby.
